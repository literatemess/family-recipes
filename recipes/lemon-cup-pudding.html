Lemon Cup Pudding

Very simple self-saucing pudding with a strong lemon flavour.

Lemon Pudding Family favourite

— serves 4 —

Ingredients

  • 1 cup caster sugar
  • 60g butter
  • 1 tbsp/⅓ cup plain flour — don't use self-raising!
  • Zest and juice of 2 lemons
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 eggs, separated
